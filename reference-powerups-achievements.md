# Power-ups & Achievements Reference (from spreadsheet)

## Power-ups

### Offensive (20 items)
1. Bounty - Place bounty on any player (Hunter receives 300pts) - 6hrs
2. Raise the Stakes - Double the existing bounty on any player (hunter receives 600pts) - 3hrs
3. Killswitch - Strip all of the target's active power-ups (destroys ALL) - N/A
4. Radar - Reveal all active players' locations on the map - 2hrs
5. Recon - See target's purchased power-ups and current point balance - 2hrs
6. Asset Freeze - Block a target from buying power-ups - 4hrs
7. Sabotage - Target's next power-up purchase costs double - 6hrs
8. Sniper's Duel - Challenge any active player to a duel. Winner gets 100pts & can steal one power-up - N/A
9. Jackpot - Next kill earns double points (1x per game) - 24hrs
10. Bounty Hunter - Bonus points for next bounty you collect (Earns 450pts for 1 kill) - 24hrs
11. Vampire - Gain 1 extra life for every kill in the time period (Max 3 lifes / 1x per game) - 2hrs
12. Smoke Screen - Hide all map power-up pickups from every active player - 2hrs
13. Vendetta - Go after a previously missed target from prior round - 2hrs
14. Blackout - Remove all player locations from the map for certain time period - 2hrs
15. Fall Guy - Force someone to be your bodyguard, if you're shot, they're eliminated (1x per game) - 4hrs
16. Hitman's Cut - Get half the points from any kills achieved while active - 4hrs
17. Frame Job - Transfer your bounty to someone else, if they're eliminated your bounty is fulfilled - 4hrs
18. Strip Search - Remove another player's immunity shield for 2hrs - 2hrs
19. Boomerang - If your hunter shoots you while active, it will boomerang back & eliminate them - 3hrs

### Defensive (15 items)
1. Immunity Shield - Immunity from elimination while active - 4hrs
2. Dead Zone - Hide location from the map while active - 2hrs
3. Clean Slate - Remove a bounty placed on you - N/A
4. Radar Detector - Get notified when someone checks your location on the map - 4hrs
5. Revive - Come back to life after an elimination. Must be used in round that elimination occurred. (1x per game) - N/A
6. Untouchable - 24hr Immunity (Only 1x per game) - 24hrs
7. Lucky Charm - Auto-survive your next elimination without a re-entry fee - N/A
8. Decoy - Drop a fake location marker that others think is you - 2hrs
9. Identity Theft - Swap map location with any active player - 2hrs
10. Mirror, Mirror - Steal power of someone else's active power-up and get the effects applied to you - 2hrs
11. Bodyguard - Give another player protection for a time. If they get shot you lose 150pts. (1x per game) - 3hrs
12. Respawn - Undo your elimination, must pay half of the revival fee. Must be used within 1hr - N/A
13. Witness Protection - Get temporary safety from elimination and location removed from the map - 2hrs
14. Sanctuary - Safe location for 2hrs. You can be eliminated but they may not enter the location to do it - 2hrs
15. Burner Phone - Location power-ups used against will not work (Trace, Radar, Smoke Screen) - 2hrs

### Chaos (9 items)
1. Safe Swap - Swap safe object for all players - 24hrs
2. Reassignment - Swap for a random new target - N/A
3. Pickpocket - Drain half the current points from a target & add to your score (Max 400) - N/A
4. Freaky Friday - Swap ALL players' targets at once for remainder of the round - N/A
5. Lifeline - Revive one eliminated player, must be used in same round (2x max per game) - N/A
6. Care Package - Gift any power-up you own to any other player - N/A
7. Open Season - Eliminate any player, no safe objects. 50pts for each elimination - 30min
8. Roulette - Spin for random power-ups, discounts and points - N/A
9. Wildcard - Choose your target for the next round - N/A
